Hindu Religious & Charitable Endowments Department

The Hindu Religious and Charitable Endowments Department manages temple administration, preserves cultural heritage, and ensures proper governance of temples across Tamil Nadu. This portal supports temple location updates, jurisdiction-based approvals, and public access to temple information. It enables authorized officials and sub users to update temple records through a secure approval workflow integrated with GIS-based location mapping.
